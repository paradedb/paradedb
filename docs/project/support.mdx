For community support and general questions, join the [ParadeDB Community Slack](https://www.paradedb.com/slack).

For enterprise support, commercial licensing, or self-hosting ParadeDB Enterprise, please [contact sales](mailto:sales@paradedb.com).

## Ask a Question

Click **Ask Assistant** in the top navigation to get answers about ParadeDB. The AI assistant uses the documentation to help with queries, troubleshooting, and best practices.
