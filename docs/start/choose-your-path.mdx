There are three ways to get ParadeDB running. Pick the one that matches where your data lives today:

- **[Quickstart with Docker](/documentation/getting-started/install)**: fastest way to try ParadeDB with nothing installed.
- **[Install into existing Postgres](/deploy/self-hosted/extension)**: add the pg_search extension to a Postgres you already run.
- **[Replicate from a managed primary](/deploy/logical-replication/getting-started)**: keep your primary where it is and stream into ParadeDB.
